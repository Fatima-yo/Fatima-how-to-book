
======================
Creating Qubes
======================

1. open terminal in dom0

2. see existing qubes
qvm-ls debian-13-xfce
qvm-ls debian13xfceCustom

3. clone a template
qvm-clone debian-13-xfce debian13xfceName
qvm-clone debian-13-xfce debian13xfceCustom
qvm-clone debian13xfceCustom debian13xfceName
