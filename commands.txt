
=================================
dom0 useful commands
=================================

move file from dom0 to a qube
-----------------------------
qvm-move-to-vm fatima /home/fatima/Desktop/error.txt


move file from qube to dom0
---------------------------
qvm-run -p fatima 'cat /home/user/Downloads/FATIMA-WORK/Linux-Software/unman.asc' > unman.asc


delete, create, edit, check file, and move it to a qube
-------------------------------------------------------
rm /home/fatima/Desktop/error.txt
touch /home/fatima/Desktop/error.txt
nano /home/fatima/Desktop/error.txt
cat /home/fatima/Desktop/error.txt
qvm-copy-to-vm fatima /home/fatima/Desktop/error.txt
